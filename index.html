About the artist

Based in Brighton, painting what matters.

I’m a Brighton-based artist creating bespoke paintings of people, pets and meaningful moments. My commissions are made to feel personal rather than posed — capturing expression, character and the quiet details that make a subject instantly recognisable to the people who love them.

From joyful gifts to memorial paintings, each piece is individually made and designed to become part of your home and your family story.
